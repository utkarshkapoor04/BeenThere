Big decisions, very little real information

At 15, most students choose a stream based on marks, relatives' opinions and what their friends are picking.

“Science, Commerce or Arts?”

The choice gets made before most students have met anyone who works in the field.

Advice comes second-hand

Relatives and coaching centres share what they've heard, not what the job is like day to day.

Changing course is costly

Plenty of people switch paths after years: law to finance, engineering to design. Earlier conversations would have saved them time.

How a circle works

No lectures and no sales pitch. Just a few honest conversations with someone a few steps ahead of you.

Tell us what you're curious about

Design, law, medicine, startups, civil services: pick one or two fields you want to explore.

Join a small circle

We match you with up to five students and one mentor who works in that field. Mentors are experienced professionals, not students.

Ask everything

Over a few sessions you learn what the work involves, how they got there, and what they'd do differently.
